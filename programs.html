Frequently Asked Questions

For more information about available programs, entry requirements, or tuition options, please contact Openway Consultancy.

1 - What are the most popular programs in Malaysia?

Popular choices include business, accounting, engineering, computer science, hospitality, health sciences, and foundation pathways.

2 - Can international students study in English in Malaysia?

Yes. Many universities in Malaysia offer English-taught programs, especially for international students.

We help you choose based on your academic background, career interests, budget, and long-term plans.

Yes. Malaysia offers foundation, diploma, undergraduate, and postgraduate study options across many fields.

Yes. We can help you compare program options across universities, including tuition ranges, entry criteria, and available pathways.
