Study Levels

Every academic level, explained clearly

Whether you are entering university for the first time or continuing to postgraduate study, Malaysia has an entry point for your background.

Foundation

A one-year preparatory program designed for students who have completed secondary school (O-Level / SPM) and want to progress to a bachelor's degree.

  • Duration: 1 year
  • Entry: O-Level / SPM or equivalent
  • Leads to: Undergraduate degree
Diploma

A 2€“3 year professional qualification that can serve as a standalone credential or a pathway to top up into a bachelor's degree program.

  • Duration: 2€“3 years
  • Entry: O-Level / SPM or equivalent
  • Leads to: Career or degree top-up
Undergraduate

A 3€“4 year bachelor's degree program with international recognition. The most common level for students entering university directly or after foundation/diploma.

  • Duration: 3€“4 years
  • Entry: Foundation, A-Level, or equivalent
  • Leads to: Bachelor's degree
Postgraduate

Master's and PhD programs for graduates who want to deepen expertise, specialize in a field, or advance their professional standing.

  • Duration: 1€“3 years
  • Entry: Bachelor's degree
  • Leads to: Master's or PhD
Program Categories

Popular fields of study in Malaysia

Malaysia's universities offer a broad range of programs. Below are the most in-demand fields for international students.

Business & Management

One of the most popular choices for international students. Programs cover marketing, finance, human resources, entrepreneurship, and international business.

  • Available at: Foundation, Diploma, Undergraduate, Postgraduate
  • English-medium instruction available
Information Technology

A fast-growing field with strong employment prospects. Programs include computer science, software engineering, cybersecurity, data science, and networking.

  • Available at: Diploma, Undergraduate, Postgraduate
  • High demand field in Malaysia and globally
Engineering

Covers civil, mechanical, electrical, electronics, and chemical engineering. Programs are typically 4 years at the undergraduate level and are professionally accredited.

  • Available at: Foundation, Undergraduate, Postgraduate
  • Accredited programs with industry links
Hospitality & Tourism

Malaysia's growing tourism industry makes this a practical and career-ready field. Programs cover hotel management, event planning, culinary arts, and tourism operations.

  • Available at: Diploma, Undergraduate
  • Strong internship and industry connections
Health Sciences

Includes pharmacy, physiotherapy, biomedical science, optometry, nursing, and pre-medical pathways. Entry requirements are typically higher for health programs.

  • Available at: Foundation, Undergraduate, Postgraduate
  • Strict entry requirements €” we can guide you
Design & Creative Arts

Programs in graphic design, interior design, architecture, mass communication, and media. Ideal for students with a creative focus and strong portfolio ambitions.

  • Available at: Diploma, Undergraduate
  • Portfolio may be required at some universities
