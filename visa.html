Common Questions

What students usually ask about visa support

Here are some of the most common questions students have before getting started.

Common documents include a valid passport, your university offer or acceptance letter, academic transcripts and certificates, a passport-sized photograph, proof of financial support, and a medical examination report from an approved clinic. Requirements can vary depending on your nationality and the university you are enrolling in. We help you compile and review your specific document set before submission.

In most cases, the Malaysia student pass process is handled through the university once the student has received their offer letter. Processing time can range from 4 to 12 weeks depending on the university and the student's country of origin. We advise students to start the process early and avoid last-minute document preparation, which is one of the most common causes of delays.

Yes, in Malaysia the student pass application is submitted by the university through the Education Malaysia Global Services (EMGS) system. The student provides the required documents, and the university manages the submission. We guide you on what to prepare and how to avoid common document errors that slow down the process.

A Visa Approval Letter (VAL) is issued once your student pass application is approved through EMGS. Students from countries that require a visa to enter Malaysia typically need this letter before traveling. Students from visa-exempt countries may enter on a social visit pass and convert it after arrival. We advise you on the exact process based on your nationality.
